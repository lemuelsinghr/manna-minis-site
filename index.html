Manna Minis pipeline

An internal tool for the Manna Minis YouTube channel.

Manna Minis makes short Bible quiz videos for children aged 5 to 10, in English and Tamil. The questions come from the American Standard Version of the Bible, which is in the public domain.

This pipeline is the channel owner's own tool for making those videos. It prepares the quiz questions, narration and animation, saves the finished files in the owner's Google Drive, keeps a review list in the owner's Google Sheets, and uploads finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel as private videos. The owner reviews every video and publishes it by hand.

It is not a public app. No one else signs in to it, and it does not access anyone else's account.
