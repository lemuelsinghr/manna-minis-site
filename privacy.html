Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The Manna Minis pipeline is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Manna Minis YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given once through Google sign-in, it accesses only the owner's own Google account:

  • Google Drive: to save and read the channel's video files, thumbnails and working files.
  • Google Sheets: to keep the owner's review list (which questions to keep, proofreading, approvals).
  • YouTube: to upload the channel's own videos as private, set their thumbnails, and check whether the owner has published them.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about any other person, including viewers of the channel.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.

Storage and removal

Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive and YouTube channel. The sign-in token is stored only as an encrypted secret for the owner's own automation. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos uploaded to YouTube are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the owner through github.com/lemuelsinghr.
